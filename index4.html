Our Heritage

A Legacy of Royal Indian Gastronomy

Rooted in the royal kitchens of Awadh and Mughal emperors, Riyasat was founded with a passion to revive centuries-old culinary traditions. Our master khansamas handcraft each delicacy using time-honored slow-cooking methods ('Dum Pukht') in sealed copper handis over fragrant charcoal embers.

Infused with pure Kashmiri saffron, stone-ground garam masalas, and heritage aromatics, every meal at Riyasat is an unforgettable poetic celebration of regal Indian hospitality.
