A Legacy of Royal Indian Gastronomy

The Heritage of Zaffran

Royal Origins

Honouring Regal Culinary Traditions

Rooted in the royal courts of Awadh, Rajasthan, and Mughal India, Zaffran is a celebration of centuries-old gastronomy. Our journey began with a singular devotion: to revive forgotten recipes, slow-braised delicacies, and hand-ground spice alchemy that once graced imperial banquets.

Every creation at Zaffran is crafted with pure Grade-A Kashmiri saffron, organic hand-churned Vedic ghee, and stone-ground spices. Our culinary artisans embrace the art of 'Dum Pukht'—sealing pots with dough to gently infuse aromas over glowing charcoal embers.
